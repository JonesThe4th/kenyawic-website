Kenya Women Investment Company — Corporate Website

Live site: kenyawic.com

Corporate website for the Kenya Women Investment Company (KWIC), an all-women savings and investment corporation founded in Nairobi in 2006. KWIC pools member capital for investment in real estate, equities, and money markets, with a membership of professional and businesswomen across Nairobi.

Project Brief

The client needed a professional, trust-building digital presence that reflects their institutional credibility, communicates their governance structure and strategic focus areas, and serves as a platform for shareholder communications and external visibility.

What Was Built
Responsive corporate website optimised for desktop and mobile
Clean, professional visual design aligned with KWIC's brand identity
Sections covering company background, membership, governance, and strategic focus areas
Image integration and content management for ongoing updates
SEO-optimised structure for discoverability
Tech & Approach

Built with a focus on performance, clarity, and institutional credibility. Designed to reflect the professionalism of a governed investment company rather than a personal or startup brand.
